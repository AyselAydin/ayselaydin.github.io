Privacy Policy

Hangman - Classic Word Game
Last Updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Hangman - Classic Word Game ("Hangman"), developed by Aysel Aydin.

1. Information We Collect

Hangman does not require you to create an account and does not directly request or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Game-related information, such as progress, scores, earned hints, or unlocked content, may be stored locally on your device. This locally stored game data is not intentionally transmitted by Hangman to our servers.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Hangman may use third-party services, including Google AdMob, to display advertisements. These services may automatically collect or process certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, general location derived from IP address, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and other technical information, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable platform settings.

The types of information processed by third-party services may vary depending on your device, operating system, region, privacy choices, consent status, and the configuration of those services.

For more information about how Google handles information when its services are used, please review Google's privacy information and policies.

3. Analytics and Diagnostics

Third-party services integrated into the app may process technical, diagnostic, crash, performance, or usage information to provide, maintain, secure, and improve their services and the app experience.

4. Children's Privacy

Hangman does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Where required, advertising and third-party services are configured in accordance with applicable platform requirements and privacy laws.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

5. Device Permissions

Hangman does not require access to sensitive device features such as your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location for its core gameplay.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, depending on your operating system.

Information processed independently by third-party service providers is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.
